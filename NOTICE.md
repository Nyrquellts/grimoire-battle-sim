## What is in this repository

- **Original work:** the source code, tests, tooling, interface, layout, writing and documentation.
  This is what the [MIT license](LICENSE) covers.
- **Game-mechanics data:** numeric tables and rule parameters — creature statistics, weapon and
  armour values, costs, skill lists — modelled on the combat system of a tabletop role-playing game.
  Rules and numbers describe how a game plays; they are not reproduced here as prose.
- **Setting vocabulary:** the names of monsters, Signs, potions, regions and similar terms, used
  the way any bestiary, character sheet or fan tool uses them: to label things.

## What is not

No rulebook text, flavour text, artwork, maps, fonts, logos or other publisher-created expression is
included. Descriptions, narration, scenario briefings, dialogue and tooltips are original wording.
No book scans, PDFs or extraction output are part of the repository. The premade heroes are
original characters, and the names the generators produce come from invented or common-use name pools.

## Replacing the setting

Content is data: every creature, weapon, Sign, scenario and battlefield is a registry row that a
content pack can add or replace without touching engine code. Anyone forking the engine for a
setting of their own can swap the data wholesale. See [docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md](docs/CONTENT_POLICY.md).
